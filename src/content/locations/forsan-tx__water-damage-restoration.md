## Why Forsan Properties See Water Damage Issues

Most homes and outbuildings in and around Forsan sit on slab foundations poured into expansive clay soil, the same caliche-heavy ground found across Howard County. When that soil gets saturated from a burst pipe, an overflowing water heater, or a hard West Texas thunderstorm, it swells and shifts, which can push water sideways under slabs instead of letting it drain away. Combine that with older galvanized plumbing still in service in a lot of the area's farmhouses and outbuildings, and a slow pinhole leak behind a wall can run for days before anyone notices the baseboards have gone soft. Wind-driven rain during spring storm season also tends to find its way in through roof penetrations and window flashing on older construction, which turns a roof problem into a water problem fast.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Forsan

The first visit is about stopping the loss and reading the moisture, not just running a shop vac over wet carpet. Structural drying equipment goes in next, calibrated for Howard County's low humidity and high daytime heat, which actually works in our favor for surface drying but can mask moisture that's still trapped in subfloor or wall cavities.
