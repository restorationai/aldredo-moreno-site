Much of the housing stock around Ackerly dates back several decades, with slab foundations poured over the area's heavy clay soil. ## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Ackerly

We start with extraction, pulling standing water out with pumps and truck-mount equipment before it has a chance to wick further into baseboards and subflooring. From there we check moisture levels in the walls and framing, not just the surface, because a slab home can look dry on top while the insulation inside an exterior wall stays saturated for days. Structural drying follows the same general approach outlined in the IICRC S500 standard: set air movers and dehumidifiers to pull moisture out at a controlled rate so wood framing and drywall dry evenly instead of warping or delaminating. For Ackerly homes with original hardwood or older vinyl flooring, we adjust drying times accordingly since these materials release moisture more slowly than newer engineered products. We document everything with moisture readings and photos as we go, which matters later if a claim needs to show the extent of the loss.
