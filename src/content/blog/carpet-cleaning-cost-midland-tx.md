## How Much Does Carpet Cleaning Cost in Midland, TX?

Most Midland homes pay between $120 and $350 for a whole-house cleaning, or $40 to $75 for a single room, depending on square footage, soil level, and method. Per-square-foot pricing typically runs $0.25 to $0.50.

| Scenario | Typical Cost Range |
|---|---|
| Single room (up to 200 sq ft), hot water extraction | $40 - $75 |
| Whole house, 3-4 bedrooms (~1,500-1,800 sq ft) | $150 - $350 |
| Per-square-foot rate (most companies) | $0.25 - $0.50 / sq ft |
| Stairs, per flight (12-16 steps) | $30 - $60 added |
| Pet stain and odor treatment, per area | $50 - $150 added |
| Heavy traffic lane or deep soil pre-treatment | $0.10 - $0.20 / sq ft added |
| Furniture moving, per room | $25 - $50 added |

Companies price small jobs as a flat per-room rate and larger jobs by square footage, usually with a minimum service charge somewhere around $75-$100 so a single hallway doesn't cost less than the trip itself.

## What's the Difference Between Hot Water Extraction and Low-Moisture Cleaning, Cost-Wise?

For a routine maintenance cleaning, hot water extraction and low-moisture cleaning cost about the same per square foot. The real difference is dry time and what each method handles best, not price.

Hot water extraction, often called steam cleaning, uses pressurized hot water and a wand or truck-mounted unit to flush soil out of the carpet fibers. It's the better choice for heavily soiled carpet, allergy-sensitive households, or carpet that hasn't been deep-cleaned in over a year, but it takes 6 to 12 hours to fully dry. Low-moisture cleaning (encapsulation or bonnet methods) uses a fraction of the water and dries in 1 to 2 hours, which makes it a good fit for light maintenance cleanings or homes that need the floor walkable again quickly. It's less effective on deep, ground-in soil. Pick the method based on how dirty the carpet actually is, not whichever one sounds cheaper on the phone.

## What Adds to the Price?

Stairs, pet stains, heavy traffic lanes, and furniture moving are the most common line items that push a quote above the base per-room or per-square-foot rate.

- **Stairs** are priced per step or per flight because they take longer to work by hand, not by wand: expect $30 to $60 per flight.
- **Pet stains and odor** need enzyme or oxidizing pre-treatment that a standard cleaning pass won't remove: $50 to $150 per affected area, depending on how far the stain has soaked into the pad.
- **Heavy traffic lanes** (hallways, in front of a couch, a well-worn path to the kitchen) need a pre-spray and extra dwell time before extraction: $0.10 to $0.20 per square foot on top of the base rate.
- **Furniture moving** (sofas, beds, dressers) adds labor time: $25 to $50 per room, and most companies won't move electronics, pianos, or anything on casters without a waiver.

Ask for these line items up front. A quote that looks low on the phone and high on the invoice almost always comes down to one of these four.

## Is a $99 Whole-House Carpet Special Worth It?

A $99 whole-house special is usually worth it only if you read what's actually included, because most are capped at a set number of rooms or square feet and leave out stairs, pet treatment, and heavy-soil pre-treatment entirely.

The $99 number is typically an advertised starting price for up to three or four average-sized rooms with light soil and no extras. The moment your home has a fifth bedroom, a set of stairs, a dog, or carpet that hasn't been cleaned in two years, the technician will quote add-ons that can bring the total closer to $250-$350 anyway. That's not necessarily a bait-and-switch, it's how the math works when deep cleaning takes real time and chemical. Before booking, ask for the out-the-door price for your actual square footage and condition, not just the headline number.

## How Often Should You Clean Carpet in a Dusty West Texas Home?

Most Midland-area homes need carpet cleaned every 9 to 12 months, sooner than many other regions, because wind-blown Permian Basin dust and caliche settle deep into carpet fibers between visits.

Vacuuming alone doesn't pull fine mineral dust out of the pile once it works below the surface. That buildup is part of what the [EPA tracks as indoor air quality](https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq), since settled dust in flooring and fabric gets stirred back into the air with every footstep. Homes with pets, high foot traffic, or windows left open during spring dust events often do better on a 6 to 9 month cycle rather than waiting the full year.

## When Does Wet Carpet Need Water Damage Drying Instead of a Cleaning?

Carpet that's wet from a supply line leak, appliance failure, or backup needs water damage extraction and structural drying, not a routine cleaning, because standing water that sits more than 24 to 48 hours lets bacteria and odor take hold in the padding underneath.

A cleaning technician's wand pulls surface moisture; it's not built to dry a saturated pad or check the subfloor for trapped water. If the source was a clean supply line and the water is caught within a day, the carpet may be salvageable with commercial air movers and dehumidification. If the water came from a drain or sewer backup, the padding and often the carpet itself usually need to be removed rather than dried, since that water carries contamination that a cleaning pass can't resolve.
