## Restoration emergencies common in Ackerly

Ackerly sits out on the Caprock plains between Big Spring and Lamesa, and the water problems here tend to follow the weather and the land. Summer thunderstorms can drop a lot of rain fast on ground that doesn't absorb it quickly, which means water finds its way into crawlspaces and low-lying slabs before anyone notices. Winter brings hard freezes that catch exposed or poorly insulated plumbing, and a burst pipe in an outbuilding or an older farmhouse can run for hours before it's found.
