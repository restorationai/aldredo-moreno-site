A broken window, a shattered patio door or a damaged entry door leaves a home or business open to the weather and to anyone passing by. Emergency board up closes that opening fast with plywood cut to fit and fastened securely, so the building is protected until the glass, door or wall can be properly repaired. ACS Enterprise provides board up for homes and businesses in Midland and the surrounding communities.

## What we board up

Board up is about the openings in your walls:

- **Broken windows** from hail, wind-blown debris, a stray rock or a break-in.
- **Sliding glass and patio doors** that have shattered or will no longer lock.
- **Entry and garage side doors** that were forced, damaged or knocked off their frame.
- **Holes in exterior walls** left by storm debris or a vehicle.

ACS Enterprise does not do roofing or roof tarping. If the roof is damaged, a roofer handles the roof; we secure the windows, doors and wall openings and take care of any water that got inside.

## Our process

1. **Safety check.** We look over the opening and the area around it, clear loose glass and debris from the frame, and make sure the space is safe to work in.

2. **Photos before we cover anything.** The damage is photographed as found, so you have a record for your insurance company before the plywood goes up.

3. **Cut to fit.** Plywood is measured and cut to the size of each opening rather than leaning a loose sheet against it.

4. **Fasten it securely.** Each board is fastened to the frame or the surrounding structure so it holds against wind and keeps people out.

5. **Next steps.** You get a plain account of what was secured and what still needs repair. If rain or a burst line got inside, we can also handle the water cleanup and drying.

## Why board up matters right away

An open window or door does not stay a small problem for long. The next rain soaks the floor and walls inside, blowing dust coats everything, and an unsecured opening invites theft and animals. Securing it quickly limits the damage to the opening itself, which keeps the repair smaller and makes the insurance claim simpler.

## Board up after storms and break-ins

In the Permian Basin, hail and high winds are the most common reasons windows and doors break, and those storms often bring rain right behind them. Board up is usually the first step of our [storm damage restoration](/services/storm-damage-restoration/) work: secure the opening, then deal with any water that came in through it with [water damage restoration](/services/water-damage-restoration/). After a break-in or vandalism, board up keeps the property closed until the door or glass is replaced, and we can haul off the broken glass and debris with our [junk and debris removal](/services/junk-debris-removal/) service.
